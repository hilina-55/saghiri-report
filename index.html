💡 Main Insights
🌡️

Sudden Fever: The Primary Concern

Sudden fever is the most common worry for mothers (168 of 543). This makes real-time monitoring and instant alerts one of the most important features for the app.

📱

Strong interest in the smart app

93.1% of participants are ready to use the app or are thinking about it. This shows real market demand for a complete tech solution to monitor child health.

💊

Forgotten Medication: The Most Common Challenge

25.8% of mothers forgot medicine doses. This shows a real need for a smart, reliable reminder system built into the app.

🎵

Baby Cry Analysis: A Highly Requested Feature

78% of participants want a cry analysis feature to understand their baby's needs without exhausting the mother. This confirms it should be a core feature in the app.

👩‍⚕️

Traditional Monitoring Still common

53% of mothers monitor their child's health by themselves, and 35% with a doctor. This shows a big opportunity for tech solutions that support, not replace, traditional care.
